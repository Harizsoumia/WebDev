University of Science and Technology Houari Boumediene

The University of Science and Technology Houari Boumediene (USTHB) was created in 1974.

This university hosts several thousand students and includes numerous faculties specialized in science and technology.

It is one of the main centers for scientific education and research in Algeria, particularly in the fields of computer science, mathematics, engineering sciences, physics, and biology.
