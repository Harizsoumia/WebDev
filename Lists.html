Skills sought by companies in web development

  • Proficiency in HTML / CSS and JavaScript
  • Experience with Front-end frameworks (React, Angular, Vue.js)
  • Knowledge of back-end frameworks (Laravel, Django, Spring)
  • Database management (PostgreSQL, Oracle, SQL Server)

Ordered Lists

Website creation process in a company

  1. Analysis of client needs and specifications
  2. Design of wireframes validated by the client
  3. Front-end and back-end development as a team
  4. Quality testing and client acceptance
  5. Deployment to production and user training

Definitions

Professional Development Environment

In a company, web development is part of a collaborative approach using agile methodologies (Scrum, Kanban). Daily exchanges between developers, project managers, and clients ensure the quality and relevance of deliverables.

Ongoing Maintenance

After deployment, technical teams ensure ongoing maintenance: bug fixes, security updates, and the addition of new features requested by the client company to adapt to their changing needs.
